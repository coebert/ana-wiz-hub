## Acceptance criteria for each batch
- Establish teaching from accessible reputable original documents before writing; distinguish physical models, clinical evidence and local practice.
- Explain mechanisms, assumptions, clinical applications, pitfalls and evidence limitations; avoid padding short introductions.
- Link sources beside relevant claims and in each topic's curated reference list. Exact excerpts are never invented or silently shortened.
- Preserve topic quizzes and existing diagrams; add learning checks alongside expanded teaching where useful.
- Check targeted tests, citation matching, browser rendering and inclusion in the private audit corpus.
- Never describe the curriculum as complete while any section remains pending.
