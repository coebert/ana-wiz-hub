The learning content is available free at anaesthesiacore.app. Choosing the one-off paid iPhone app helps support keeping AnaesthesiaCore up to date and freely accessible. No subscription or in-app purchase is required.

Privacy in the iPhone app

This information covers the first iPhone edition of AnaesthesiaCore, maintained by Dr Rob Coe. Website accounts and features are separate. The iPhone app does not ask you to create an account or sign in.

Saved topics, recent reading, completed topics and sections, study time, study-plan choices, quiz review progress and display preferences are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. The app does not upload these study records to an AnaesthesiaCore account. Your device’s backup and restore settings are separate from the app.

The iPhone app has no advertising and does not send the website’s visitor analytics. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts or location. The first release does not include AI answers, voice viva or podcast playback.

Internet connections

Core bundled teaching pages can be read without signing in. The app uses device fonts. Some illustrations, online medicine records and published corrections are loaded from online hosting services when needed. Correction reports and online records use the Supabase-based backend connected to our Lovable project. Hosting services receive connection information such as your IP address, the requested resource and normal network headers. Backend request, error and performance logs help operate, diagnose and secure the service. This operational logging is separate from the website’s visitor analytics, which the iPhone app does not send.

Following an external reference opens a service with its own privacy practices. Apple handles your App Store purchase; this app does not collect your payment-card or bank details. Apple also manages its own device, App Store and diagnostic privacy choices.

Service providers and protection

Our hosting and database services help deliver the app’s online content and correction reports. Google Workspace Gmail receives support correspondence. These services are used for the purposes described here, with confidentiality, access-control and security safeguards. Private report access in the app’s database is restricted to authorised administrators. Connections to that backend use HTTPS.

Providers handling report or support content for us are expected to protect it consistently with this notice, limit access to delivering and securing their services, and support applicable access and deletion requests. We do not authorise the use of your reports or support correspondence for targeted advertising. Lovable’s published privacy information describes encryption and access controls and excludes app users’ database content from model training and advertising use unless the app operator instructs otherwise. Google Workspace publishes data-processing and security commitments for customer content.

See Lovable’s privacy information, its backend-log documentation and Google Workspace’s published data-processing terms. Providers may process information in countries other than your own; no UK-only or EU-only storage guarantee is made here. Their published terms describe their safeguards and relevant transfer arrangements.

Optional correction reports

If you choose to report an inaccuracy, your topic details, any quoted text, your message, any suggested correction and any optional email address you enter are sent to the AnaesthesiaCore maintainers through the Supabase-based backend connected to our Lovable project. Report records include submission and review dates. Reports help us investigate and correct the educational content; an email address lets an editor follow up.

The topic-page reporting button includes the public topic address. On the corrections page, you can enter a topic address yourself. The form does not add a separate browser-identification field, although the hosting service still receives ordinary network request headers.

A report marked for publication can appear in the public corrections list, including the topic, quoted text, your report message and an editor’s note. When a report is published, the database removes its separate contact-email field and browser-identification field. These fields are not included in the public corrections list. Identifying information entered in the message or other free-text fields could still appear in a published correction. Do not include patient information or other identifying details in the report text.

Retention and deletion

We have not set a fixed retention period or automatic age-based deletion for private correction reports or support emails. A report or message may remain after an issue is resolved until it is manually removed. Published educational correction records remain available as part of the content history; their separate contact-email and browser-identification fields are removed when published. You can ask about information you have sent, or request its removal, by emailing support@anaesthesiacore.app.

Deleting information from an active database or mailbox is separate from the provider’s recovery, backup and service-log processes. Lovable describes operational and security-log retention by the need to operate and secure its services and investigate incidents. Google Workspace also has recovery and service-deletion processes. We do not promise immediate removal of every provider-held copy or a provider-specific deadline that has not been verified for this service. This page will be updated if these arrangements change.

Your choices

You can use the learning content without submitting a report or supplying an email address. Saved topics can be removed from your library. Device storage, backups and restored copies are managed through your iPhone settings. Removing the app does not delete reports or emails you have already sent.

For a question about information you submitted, or a request to access, correct or remove it, email support@anaesthesiacore.app. Include enough detail to locate the report, such as its topic and approximate submission date. Please do not send patient information.

Help with the app

Contact support@anaesthesiacore.app for support, privacy questions or feedback. The support mailbox uses Gmail through Google Workspace. If you email us, your email address, message and any attachments are sent to that mailbox so we can respond. Send only what is needed to explain the problem. See Google’s privacy information and Google’s published Workspace data-processing terms. For a technical problem, describe what happened and, if you can, provide your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. Check any screenshot for personal or patient information before sending it.

Use “Report an inaccuracy” on a topic for a content correction. AnaesthesiaCore is an educational resource and is not a clinical decision-making or prescribing tool. It is not an emergency or patient-care support service.
