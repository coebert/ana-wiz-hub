# AnaesthesiaCore — Stage 1 App Store readiness register

Assessment date: 1 November 2026. Target: native iOS, one-time paid App Store download, Primary FRCA + Final FRCA + FFICM. No price, release date, lifetime-update promise or college endorsement assumed.

## Outcome
There is demonstrable educational value, but **submission readiness is not established**. The sampled public study journey works. Trustworthy release claims, account/privacy completeness, native delivery, independent clinical review and real-device evidence remain gates.

Only planning documents and this register were changed. No application code, clinical teaching, backend, database rows, podcasts/audio, credentials, payment settings, holds or deployment were changed. No generation or publication was performed.

Browser scripts/screenshots and test log were captured under `/tmp/browser/app-store-readiness/` for this session. Initial checks needed selector corrections: search has no dialog role; collapsible heading accessible names include extra completion controls. These harness retries were not application failures. Screenshots taken during animation were replaced with settled evidence where needed.

### Read-only safeguards and limitations
All cloud function requests and all non-local external requests with write-capable methods were blocked by browser interception; five `log-visit` POST attempts were blocked in the final study run. Public static/CDN reads were allowed. No contribution, review submission, podcast playback/regeneration or microphone action was used. The browser context was disposable and signed out.

Authenticated sign-in and saved-account flows were intentionally **not exercised** in this no-database-writes stage: progress hydration may upload local records and study-time tracking may write. Social buttons were observed, not used. Hosted auth settings, deletion behaviour, privacy retention and production telemetry remain unverified.
