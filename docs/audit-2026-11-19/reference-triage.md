## Distinction preserved

- **Measured false attribution**: the stored DOI was resolved and returned a different
  article. This is a publisher-record fact, not an inference from title similarity.
- **Unresolved lookup**: the cited work could not be located in its journal, so the entry
  is withheld rather than re-pointed at a lookalike article.
- Low title overlap alone never removed an entry: 7 candidates had automated title
  coverage below 60%% and were kept because the identifier resolved to the cited work.
